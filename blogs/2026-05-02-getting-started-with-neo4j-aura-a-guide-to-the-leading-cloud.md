<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-why-use-neo4j-cloud">Why Use Neo4j Cloud?</h3>



<p>Unlike self-managed versions, Neo4j Aura handles provisioning, patching, and upgrades automatically, significantly reducing your operational burden.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Native Graph Performance: Unlike relational databases that use slow &#8220;JOINs,&#8221; Neo4j uses index-free adjacency, making relationship traversals lightning-fast—even across billions of nodes.</li>



<li>Simple, Consumption-Based Pricing: You pay only for provisioned capacity on a predictable model, with no extra fees for storage, compute, or backups.</li>



<li>Zero Administration Overhead: AuraDB handles all management tasks, allowing your team to focus on building applications rather than managing infrastructure.</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">How to Get Started on Neo4j</h3>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Start for Free </strong><br />If you are just beginning your journey, AuraDB Free is the perfect place to learn and explore graph databases. It includes one fully functional graph database with no time limits, supporting up to 200,000 nodes and 400,000 relationships.</li>



<li><strong>Deploy via Cloud Marketplaces </strong><br />For production workloads, you can start immediately via the Neo4j Aura Console or through the AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure marketplaces. Deploying through a marketplace allows you to utilize existing cloud credits and consolidate your billing.</li>



<li><strong>Scale on Demand </strong><br />As your application grows, you can vertically scale your instances—up to 128GB RAM on Professional or 512GB RAM on Business Critical—with zero downtime.</li>



<li><strong>Import Your Data Fast </strong><br />Use the Neo4j  Data Importer, a no-code tool that lets you drag and drop CSV files to build your graph model visually. This mirrors your conceptual sketches on a whiteboard, making the transition from design to implementation seamless.</li>
</ol>
