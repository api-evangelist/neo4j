This article explains how graphs help organizations uncover relationships in fragmented data, knowledge graphs create shared business understanding across systems, and context graphs deliver task-specific AI context to improve accuracy and reduce hallucinations. As enterprises adopt generative AI and agents, connected data becomes essential for grounded, business-aware outcomes rather than plausible but potentially inaccurate responses.
